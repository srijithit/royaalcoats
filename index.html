Process
How It Works

Four Steps. One Perfect Finish.

01
Surface Preparation
Sandblasting and chemical cleaning remove all contaminants, rust, and old coatings to create the ideal bonding surface.
02
Pretreatment
Multi-stage washing and phosphate conversion coating ensures maximum adhesion and baseline corrosion protection.
03
Electrostatic Application
Charged powder wraps every edge uniformly via electrostatic spray, achieving complete and consistent coverage.
04
Cure & Quality Check
Precision oven curing fuses the powder for maximum hardness. Every piece is inspected before release.
Help & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our professional powder coating services, finishes, and turnaround in Coimbatore.

What types of metals can you powder coat in Coimbatore?

At Royaal Coats, we powder coat mild steel (MS), structural steel, sheet metal, aluminium profiles, cast iron, galvanized steel, and stainless steel components. We specialize in machinery parts, architectural fabrications, alloy wheels, and metal furniture.

Where is Royaal Coats located, and which areas do you serve?

Our facility is at 18/2A, Jaganatha Industrial Area, Chinnavedampatti, Coimbatore (behind CRI Pumps, Ganapathi). We regularly serve industrial and commercial clients from Chinnavedampatti, Ganapathy, Saravanampatti, Sivanandhapuram, Peelamedu, Kalapatti, and Vilankurichi.

What colours and finishes are available for powder coating?

We provide a comprehensive spectrum of RAL standard shades, custom colour matching, matte, gloss, semi-gloss, satin, metallic, and textured anti-slip finishes. All coatings use premium UV-resistant and corrosion-resistant powders.

Do you offer powder coating stripping and recoating?

Yes. We provide complete surface restoration, including chemical stripping, sandblasting, and old powder coating removal, followed by multi-stage pretreatment and factory-grade powder recoating for refreshed parts.

Can you handle high-volume bulk or batch orders?

Yes. Equipped with large-capacity curing ovens and multi-stage pretreatment bays, we specialize in high-volume batch powder coating and production contracts with fast turnaround times and strict quality control.

Why choose electrostatic powder coating over liquid painting?

Electrostatic powder coating provides superior scratch resistance, impact durability, corrosion protection, and uniform film thickness without drips or runs. It is eco-friendly with zero VOC emissions and outlasts liquid paint by years.
