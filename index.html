Process
How It Works

Four Steps. One Perfect Finish.

01
Surface Preparation
Sandblasting and chemical cleaning remove all contaminants, rust, and old coatings to create the ideal bonding surface.
02
Pretreatment
Multi-stage washing and phosphate conversion coating ensures maximum adhesion and baseline corrosion protection.
03
Electrostatic Application
Charged powder wraps every edge uniformly via electrostatic spray, achieving complete and consistent coverage.
04
Cure & Quality Check
Precision oven curing fuses the powder for maximum hardness. Every piece is inspected before release.
